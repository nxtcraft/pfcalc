All three schemes are EEE (Exempt-Exempt-Exempt) — contributions reduce taxable income under 80C, interest is tax-free, and maturity amount is tax-free. EPF and PPF rates are set annually; SSY and PPF rates are reviewed quarterly but have been stable for 2024-25.

How EPF Interest is Calculated — Monthly Formula

EPF interest is calculated monthly on the running balance but credited as a lump sum at year-end (March 31). The monthly rate = Annual Rate ÷ 12.

Monthly Interest Formula
Monthly Interest = Monthly Running Balance × (Annual Rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100)

At 8.25% p.a.: Monthly Rate = 8.25 ÷ 12 ÷ 100 = 0.6875%

EPF vs EPS — Employer Contribution Breakdown

The employer's 12% contribution is split between EPF and EPS. This confuses many employees who expect their employer to also contribute 12% entirely to EPF.

No — EPS ceiling is ₹15,000 × 8.33% = ₹1,250, so Employer EPF = ₹3,600 − ₹1,250 = ₹2,350). Total EPF = ₹5,950/month. Only this ₹5,950 earns 8.25% interest.
EPS gives you pension, not a lump sum. The EPS corpus funds your monthly pension after age 58 (minimum 10 years service). If you leave before 10 years, you can claim a reduced withdrawal benefit from EPS.

PPF Maturity Calculation — Compound Interest Formula

PPF uses annual compound interest. The formula: A = P × [(1+r)^n − 1] ÷ r × (1+r) for annual contributions (annuity due).

PPF — Annual Deposit, 15-Year Maturity
A = Annual deposit × [(1 + r)^n − 1] × (1 + r) ÷ r
Where: r = 0.071 (7.1%), n = 15 years

PPF Partial Withdrawal Rules

  • Partial withdrawal allowed from year 7 onwards
  • Maximum withdrawal = 50% of balance at end of year 4, or end of immediately preceding year, whichever is lower
  • Only 1 partial withdrawal per financial year
  • No TDS or tax on withdrawal

Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana — How SSY Works

SSY is a government-backed scheme exclusively for the girl child (opened before she turns 10). It offers the highest rate among all small savings schemes: 8.2% p.a. compounded annually.

Example: ₹1,00,000/year SSY at 8.2% — Maturity at Year 21 Deposits for 15 years = ₹15,00,000 invested. At 8.2% compounded annually, maturity value at year 21 ≈ ₹43.5 lakhs. The last 6 years (years 16–21) the account earns interest but no new deposits.

EEE Tax Exemption — Old vs New Tax Regime

All three schemes (EPF, PPF, SSY) are EEE — meaning tax-free at all three stages. But the deduction is only available under the old tax regime.

StageOld RegimeNew Regime (default)
Contribution (Section 80C, up to ₹1.5L)✅ Deductible❌ Not available
Interest earned✅ Tax-free✅ Tax-free
Maturity amount✅ Tax-free✅ Tax-free
Under the new regime: You lose the 80C deduction but the interest and maturity are still tax-free. EPF and PPF remain excellent investments even without 80C — but the tax benefit math changes.

The employee EPF contribution of 12% is not optional — it is deducted automatically. However, you can opt for a higher Voluntary Provident Fund (VPF) contribution beyond 12% — it also earns 8.25% and is EEE under old regime.

EPF Withdrawal Rules 2024

  • Full withdrawal: Allowed after 2 months of unemployment (after leaving a job)
  • Tax on withdrawal: Fully tax-free after 5 years of continuous service. Before 5 years: TDS 10% with PAN, 30% without PAN
  • Partial withdrawal (advance): Allowed for housing, medical emergency, education, marriage — with specific limits and conditions
  • Housing loan repayment: Up to 90% of balance after 5 years of service
  • Medical emergency: Up to 6 months' basic + DA, or 50% of employee's share
  • Online withdrawal: Available via EPFO UAN portal (uan.epfindia.gov.in) or UMANG app — Aadhaar-linked UAN required
⚠️ EPF withdrawal before age 58 is a last resort — you lose years of compound interest and permanently reduce your retirement corpus. Consider a personal loan or FD instead for short-term needs.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is EPFO's Universal Account Number (UAN)?

UAN is a 12-digit number assigned to every EPF member that remains constant throughout their career, even when changing jobs. All your PF accounts from different employers are linked under one UAN. You can track your PF balance, transfer PF between jobs, and withdraw PF online via the EPFO member portal using your UAN.

Can a self-employed person invest in PPF?

Yes. PPF is open to everyone — salaried, self-employed, or even those without income (like homemakers). You can open a PPF account at any post office or designated bank branch (SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis, Kotak, etc.). The minimum deposit is ₹500/year and maximum is ₹1.5 lakh/year.

What happens to EPF when I change jobs?

Your EPF balance can be transferred online to your new employer's PF account using the EPFO UAN portal. Use the "Transfer Claim" option. If your PF account is Aadhaar-verified, no employer approval is needed. The transfer is typically completed within 10–20 working days. Never withdraw EPF on job change — transfer it to maintain 5-year continuity and EEE status.

Is interest on EPF above ₹2.5 lakh taxable from 2021-22?

Yes. From FY 2021-22, interest earned on EPF contributions above ₹2.5 lakh/year (₹5 lakh/year for government employees where there's no employer contribution) is taxable as "income from other sources." This affects high-income employees making large VPF contributions. The standard 12% employee contribution on a salary up to ₹20,833/month stays within the ₹2.5 lakh limit.
